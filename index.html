FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions clients ask most before working with me.

How long does it take to build a website? +
Timeline depends on project scope. A landing page typically takes 3–5 days, a full SaaS application takes 3–6 weeks. I'll give you a detailed timeline estimate after reviewing your requirements.
What is your hourly rate or project pricing? +
I offer both fixed-price project quotes and hourly engagement. Fixed-price projects start at $500 for landing pages and scale up based on complexity. Contact me for a custom quote tailored to your specific needs.
Do you work with clients in the United States? +
Absolutely. Most of my clients are based in the USA. I work fully remote, adjust to US time zones for meetings and communication, and am available via email, Slack, or video call.
Will my website be SEO-optimized? +
Yes. Every website I build includes technical SEO fundamentals — semantic HTML, meta tags, Open Graph, fast load times, mobile-friendliness, and schema markup. I can also integrate with SEO tools like Google Search Console.
Do you provide post-launch support? +
Yes, I offer 30 days of complimentary support after project delivery. For ongoing maintenance, bug fixes, and feature additions, I offer monthly retainer packages to keep your site running smoothly.
Can you work with an existing codebase or team? +
Absolutely. I'm experienced in joining existing projects, reviewing code, and collaborating with development teams. I follow Git workflows, use proper branching strategies, and write clear documentation.
